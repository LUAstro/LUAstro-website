The Space Pirate Game - Rules

The Space Pirate Game is an activity where each player, on their game worksheet, places a set of symbols and values in a 7x7 blank grid. The grid is labeled with Greek letters α-η across the columns and numbers 1-7 down the rows.

Cells are selected at random unless a player gets the "Choose Next Square" symbol, which allows them to pick the next cell. When a cell is selected, everyone crosses off that grid reference and receives the symbol or value in that cell. If a player gets a value, they add it to their score, keeping a running total throughout the game. If they get a symbol, they perform the action associated with it.

Fill the grid with the following tiles: 1 x 5000 points, 2 x 3000 points, 10 x 1000 points, the special symbols listed below, and fill the rest of the grid with 10 x 200 point tiles.

Symbols and Their Actions:

  • Boarding Action: Steal another player's points.
  • Fire Lasers: Reset another player's score to zero.
  • Gift: Give 1000 points to a chosen player.
  • Swap: Swap scores with another player.
  • Raise Shields: Block one attack against you.
  • Deflectors Online: Reflect one attack back to the attacker.
  • 2X: Double your current score.
  • Anti-matter Containment Fault: Reset your score to zero.
  • B (Bank): Bank your current score (safe from future attacks).
  • Choose Next Square: You choose the next grid reference to be selected.

Use the grid PDF to play the game.
