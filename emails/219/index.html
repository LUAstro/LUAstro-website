LUAstro- Halloween Special Social (with LUPhys)!

This email was created with the campaign name: Halloween Collab

This email was created with the purpose: Halloween Collab (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 26/10/2025 to all 130 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello Astronomer!

Our event for this week is a Pumpkin Carving Social with LUPhys on Wednesday (29/10/25) at 7pm in Faraday Lecture Theatre. Tickets are £3 each, and can be purchased by completing this Google form: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfPcuPapEOJNnP3Apj70-O5a9_tvagWRVcFwxuLeHX5F-inww/viewform?usp=dialog.

There are only 14 tickets available, and the form will close once they are all purchased. If you do not manage to get a place or simply would like to try something different, do not fear! We have another free event that will be running in parallel on the same date - more details for that soon!

Clear Skies, and Happy Halloween!

- LUAstro
