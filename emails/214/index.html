Hello astronomer!

Our first session will be this Wednesday (08) at 19:00 in the Cavendish lecture theatre. There will be an accessible quiz with fun prizes, and a brief introductory talk from Dr. Mat Smith.


New posters will also be handed out to those with official memberships, with spares available for purchase.

We hope to see you there! :)
