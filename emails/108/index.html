LUAstro - Hoodies tickets

This email was created with the campaign name: hoodies

This email was created with the purpose: tell people about hoodies (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 10/02/2022 to all 160 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi everyone

Just to keep you all in the loop, we finally have a way of ordering the hoodies! The way this is all going to work is a bit complicated, so I'll try and lay out how what you lot will need to do:

1. Tomorrow, we will create an event on our SU webpage where you can buy tickets. This ticket will be free, and is a placeholder for your hoodie. If you would like a hoodie, please order one of these free tickets. This ticket will also include some info about the name you would like on the front of the hoodie and the size of hoodie you would like

2. Once we have the total number of hoodies we need to order, we will place the order with the hoodie company and send them all the info on hoodies.

3. We then get the hoodies through the mail and once everyone is happy with the hoodies and they are the design we wanted, there will be another ticket system. This ticket will cost the price of the hoodie which will be sent to the hoodie company.

Sorry this is all slightly convoluted, but we need to gauge the total number of people who want to buy a hoodie. The hoodie company we are using have deals based on the number of hoodies, which will bring down the total cost for everyone. With the 2nd ticket, it is important that if you reserved a ticket, you purchase a 2nd ticket. We will try and get in contact with people so we can send off the total amount of money Hoodies should be ~£24, but again this will vary based on the number of people who reserve hoodies.

If you are coming observing tonight, skies are looking good so we will meet at 7pm outside the Physics building.

Thanks everyone, sorry this was a slightly longer message! If you have any questions about all this, please dm us on socials (@luastrosoc) or email me 

Jacob

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
