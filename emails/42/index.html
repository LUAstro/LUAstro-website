This email was sent on the 30/10/2019 to All 73 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi {{user_forename}}!

Tomorrow we are back to normal after last weeks cancellation, and, ahead of the upcoming transit of Mercury, we will be taking a closer look at transits, eclipses, and occultations. This will be in the usual place, Welcome Center LT3 @7pm. We are not expecting clear skies, so unfortunately there will be no observations - though you may be pleased to find out we are so close to being able to actually use that 11" telescope we struggled to use last time. 

If you cannot attend the meeting for any reason that is entirely fine, but, if you want to find out more about the upcoming transit, we have information in an article on our LUSU page. Currently the plan is to be in Alexandra Square throughout the entire event, though this may yet change - please check our social media for updates!
