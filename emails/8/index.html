This email was sent on the 35 to including users belongs to all of the user groups : Alumni--Astronomy Society (LUAstroSoc)--Group 35 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi {{user_forename}}!

You are receiving this email because you have been previously registered as a member of the Astronomy Society for academic year 2017/18. 

We think you may be interested in joining the society again for this year. We will be meeting weekly on Thursdays for observation sessions, movie nights, quizzes, and more! This Thursday 18th at 7pm in Welcome Centre LT3, we will be meeting for the first time this year. We have plans for a quick introduction, meeting the PhD students (if they are available to come, they have not confirmed as of yet), a Kahoot Quiz (with prizes!) and, with any luck, an hour or so of meteor spotting at the BBQ area behind Bigforth Barn- weather permitting. 

The observatory is currently out of use, however there is good reason as the Physics Dept. is currently in the midst of upgrading the observatory and is expected to be open to us within about a month. We will have access to all the equipment owned by the Physics Dept for Astronomy. We apologise for a lot of the problems with the society last year. Of course the observatory had fallen into a state of disrepair, we were always thwarted by the weather, and had unpredictable days of meeting due to LUSU not allowing us a chance to book regular rooms. This year, this all seems to be fixed and everything seems to be running smoothly (famous last words!).

We hope to see you on Thursday.

Many thanks,

-Antonio, president

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
