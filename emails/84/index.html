LUAstro Meeting this Thursday

This email was created with the campaign name: LUAstro Brightest Objects

This email was created with the purpose: Meet up this Thursday 21/10 (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 19/10/2021 to all 115 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello everyone!

We've decided to hold another meeting/presentation this Thursday 7pm in Frankland LT. This is in the Faraday Complex next to the Physics building along the North spine. It's a much bigger room this week so hopefully we will be able to fit everyone inside. The presentation will be on the brightest objects in the night sky, and the magnitude system, which will be very useful to understand in the future

We've also created a short survey for you all to quickly fill out. We want to know what you want to do as a society, what objects you want to observe/image, so this is an easy way to collect all that data for us to sort through. There's also the opportunity to add your own ideas into consideration too

https://forms.gle/uzMJP8jzXFiVCt9z6

Just for people who haven't joined the discord, I'll put a link below again for them:

https://discord.gg/unwgmFGGvJ

Again, if you have any questions about anything, send one of the exec members a message and we'll get back to you as soon as possible

See you all Thursday!

Jacob
