LUAstro - 26/27 Executive Applications!

This email was created with the campaign name: LUAstro Hustings 26/27

This email was created with the purpose: Hustings (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 18/05/2026 to all 153 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello Astronomer!

Do you want to join the LUAstro Exec? 

Join us this Thursday (21/05/26) for Hustings in Faraday Lecture Theatre from 7pm, where we will vote on next year's Executive Committee!

If you wish to run for a role, please fill in this form!

Candidates are required to write a statement on the role they're running for. Creating a short presentation (< 5 mins) is also recommended to give you the best chance!

We hope to see you there!

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
