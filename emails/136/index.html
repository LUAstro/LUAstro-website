LUAstro Welcome Talk + Observatory Tour

This email was created with the campaign name: LUAstro first meeting

This email was created with the purpose: (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 10/10/2022 to including users belongs to any of the user groups : Current Members--Astronomy Society (LUAstroSoc)--Group 125 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello everybody! Our first meeting for this year will be in Cavendish Lecture Theatre this Wednesday at 7pm! Use the link at the top of our linktree if you want to see where that is: https://linktr.ee/luastrosoc

We'll tell you this year's plans for the society, give an introduction on astronomy, then go up to the top floor to show you the observatory, telescopes and all the other equipment we use! The max number of people allowed in the room and on the roof is 10, so we'll take people up in groups until everyone has had a look. Hope to see you there!

If you have any questions feel free to ask by emailing, or message us on any of our social media accounts (linked in the linktree). Join our Discord server to see space photos taken by some of our members! https://discord.gg/rT6tH3H4

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
