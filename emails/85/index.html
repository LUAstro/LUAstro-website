LUAstro - Meeting 28/10

This email was created with the campaign name: LUAstro: Stars

This email was created with the purpose: talk about stars (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 25/10/2021 to All 127 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello everyone!

We've got another meeting this Thursday 28/10 at 7pm in Cavendish LT. This is in the Faraday complex on the North spine and is next door but one to the lecture theatre we were in last week. This week, the presentation is on Stars and other bits! We'll talk about the lives of stars, how they work and a bit about what we can measure here on Earth.

We will also have a little update on merchandise and getting into the Observatory soon. 

If you have any questions about the meeting or anything else, please send us a message on our social media (@luastrosoc), by email or through Discord. If you aren't on the server then I'll include another link below:
https://discord.gg/Zrxz8aFU

I've heard from a few people that they haven't been able to get onto Discord properly. Nothing to worry about, we will still put out these emails so you won't be missing out on events we set up or our meetings.

See you on Thursday!

Jacob

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
