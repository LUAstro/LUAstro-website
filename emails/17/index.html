We at the Astronomy Society have organised a charity lecture series that will occur on Wednesday 12th December in Management School LT1 from 6pm onwards. It is to raise money for Lancashire Mind, a fairly local charity that aims to bring awareness of, and support those with, mental health conditions. They believe in a Lancashire where all have equal opportunities, regardless of their mental health.
