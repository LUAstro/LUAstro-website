This email was sent on the 21/10/2019 to All 66 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi {{user_forename}}!

We are expecting clear skies tonight! So tonight, an hour or so after sunset we will be cracking out the telescopes for the first time this year! Please meet us outside the Physics building (opposite Subway) at 7pm for observations involving Saturn, Uranus, and maybe catching a few meteors as tonight is also the peak of the Orionids meteor shower! It is likely we will be there until 8.30.

Since this new telescope is in early days of being configured, we do not know how well it works, and it is manual only for the time being. This means it will be very difficult to observe and photograph Uranus, but we will definitely do our best. It certainly will make it easier that it is at opposition this week!

So that's Physics Building 7pm - 8.30pm. If you turn up late please DM us on Twitter @lancasterastro, or message the Facebook page so we can send someone to let you in.
