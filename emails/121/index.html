This email was sent on the 25/03/2022 to all 168 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi everyone,

We have had a brilliant term this term, with multiple nights on everything from Dyson Swarms to Kerbal Space Program. A huge thanks to everyone who has been turning up, despite the stress of work and all the other great things.

The highlight of the term, however, has been the observing nights, where we have got some awesome images of the cosmos (see out discord for images), often turning up for multiple times per week. Because of this, the majority of events next term will be observing nights, so I hope to see you there (remeber, there is no limit on how many people can turn up to these events now) And the best part is, we are only just getting started!

The main exception to this is going to be on Wednesday, April 27th 2022, for the Astrosoc hustings.
