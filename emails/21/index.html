This email was sent on the 13/01/2019 to All 82 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi {{user_forename}}!

I hope you had a great time off, and got up to plenty of stargazing! This term we have a series of presentations planned following the entire lifetime of a star and the Universe, a movie night, a games night and a lunar eclipse! We will also be attempting weekly field observations, and getting up to the observatory more frequently if we can. So this term, we encourage you to bring your own telescope - though do be safe about it, we can't be liable for breakages! I'd recommend anyone bringing a telescope invest in a wheeled duffel bag, similar to this one here ensuring you get the correct size for your telescope and tripod.

Our weekly meet-ups will be in a different location: Thursdays @7pm in Welcome Center LT3.

, Thursdays @7pm in Welcome Center LT3.,

We hope to see you on Thursday, where we have a kahoot and fingers crossed for clear skies.

Thanks,

-Antonio
