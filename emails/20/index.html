Astronomy Week 9 - Games Night!

This email was created with the campaign name: week 9

This email was created with the purpose: (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 04/12/2018 to all 81 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi {{user_forename}}!

On Thursday at 7pm in Bowland North Seminar Room 23 we will have our normal meeting, but this week with a twist: we're having a games night with a series of competetive challenges and a points system and prizes! We have a Kahoot quiz, rounds of Pictionary and more! Please do come along for a fun interactive session.

We would also like to plug our charity event in week 10 again. That's Wednesday 12th December 2018 for a series of mini-lectures themed on Galaxies and Black Holes by Dr John Stott, Dr Julie Wardlow, Joao Calhau and Jess Craig. You can find out more information on our fundraiser or Lancashire Mind here. Tickets cost £1 online, and we ask that you consider donating on the door :) 

Also we are starting a monthly What's On In Astronomy article online. In it we will detail what's going on and how to spot some of the most easily found celestial events. You can read the first one, which covers December through to our return in January, here.

We hope to see you on Thursday!

-Antonio
