LUAstro - Observing Week 19

This email was created with the campaign name: new observing nights

This email was created with the purpose: observing nights (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 13/03/2022 to All 163 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi everyone!

As promised, we have some more Observing nights coming up! Thursday night is looking really good, so what better time than to roll out our new style of Observing Nights! 
The nights will be made up of 2 parts:

1) Small telescopes out on campus looking at brighter objects in the Night Sky such as Star clusters, M42, the Moon and many more! (Note: galaxies and fainter objects won't be visible through these telescopes). This will run from 7pm-9pm with no limit of numbers, so nyone can come along!

2) Large Imaging telescope on top of the Physics building! We will be able to see Deep-Sky objects up to a magnitude of 15. We want to get some great photos of something so we'll only look at one target throughout the night to get the most information from it as possible. This will run from 9pm to whenever it gets cloudy, so feel free to stay as long as you want! This part of the night will have a limit on numbers as we're up in the Observatory in Physics building, so there is a form below to sign up for the Deep-Sky section.

https://forms.gle/iNA29JGnZttvf2zz8

Hope this all makes sense, this is the first time we're trying one of these nights so please come along and help with the testing! If you have anymore questions please send us a message on socials (@luastrosoc) or join our Discord for more updates!

https://discord.gg/R74QCwcR

Small update on hoodies, corrections have been made and should be here sometime next week! I'll keep everyone updated with that as more info comes in

Thanks!

Jacob

Lancaster University Astronomy Society
