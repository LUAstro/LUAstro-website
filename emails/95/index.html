Hello everyone!

Bit of an early one, but the presentation on Asteroids, Comets and Meteorites will be held in George Fox LT3 (I'll meet people in Alexandra Square 10 mins before to show people where the room is) at 7pm this Thursday. We'll also talk a bit about the rest of term and what we've got coming up.

See you all Thursday!

Jacob
