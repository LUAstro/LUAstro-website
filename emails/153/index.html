Hey everyone! In this week’s newsletter we have our upcoming events, space news and a few photos to share with you!

 

Next Meeting

Next Meeting,

On Wednesday at 8pm in Fylde LT2 we will be having a new kind of presentation! Any astro-related questions sent to this link we will try to answer…

Next week we are also hosting a General Meeting! As we talked about in our previous meeting, these meetings are designed so that the members can have more of a say in the society as a whole. 

We currently have a few topics we are going to discuss (listed below) but we also want everyone to suggest any other topics they might want to discuss. We have created a form in which you can suggest topics to us. Your responses are then fed into a channel on our Discord (link in our bios on social media) where people can react to them to ‘second’ certain motions, which will be discussed sooner than other matters. If we end up deleting a response from the channel, it’s because a similar topic has been presented, so we will make a note of it, similar to a reaction in the channel.

Nothing is off limits, so ask away! If a vote is required, we can hold one at the meeting. In order for the decisions made there to be as fair as possible, we would love for as many people as possible to come!

The topics we currently have listed for discussion are (in no particular order):

Honorary Members

Potential logo redesign

Hoodies

 

LY:MAN Alpha Update

LY:MAN Alpha Update,

Unfortunately it looks like there won't be any clear nights this week, but if there are and we're holding an observing night, we'll let you know!

In the meantime, we need to vote on what targets we want! We had a short discussion at the end of our last meeting, where we laid out a few possible options for people to pick from. These have been compiled into each category, and added to a google form so everyone can vote on them! When it comes to the observing sessions, this will be a big part of the evening as we want to get as much good data from these targets as possible. Check out the form for the potential targets we’re thinking of, and feel free to suggest some others!

Jodrell Bank Trip

Jodrell Bank Trip,

It is finally here! On March 19th at 10am, we are going to the Jodrell Bank, a UNESCO world heritage radio telescope just outside of Manchester. It is the world's third largest steerable dish radio telescope, instrumental in the discovery of cosmic rays, meteoroids, quasars, pulsars, masers and gravitational lenses, and was heavily involved with the tracking of space probes at the start of the Space Age.

The day will involve driving there for 11:30am, to see the exhibits and the telescope in all its glory. Exhibits include the new First Light Discovery centre, highlighting the history of the telescope, several exhibits on cosmology, an expansive grounds with a scale solar system and a clockwork orrery (and, of course, two cafes). We will be home in Lancaster by 6 at the latest, but if there is a consensus to go before that, we can be back earlier. 

If you are interested, fill out the form here (total cost is a tenner)! If you have a car and are willing to drive people, let us know.

 

Photo gallery

Photo gallery,

This is a new photo from JWST showing the side of M92, a globular cluster in our galaxy! JWST managed to take photos of each side of the cluster, but wasn’t able to capture the middle, since the same exposure times used on the dimmer outskirts would blow out the image of the centre. In the full image, there is instead a black strip in its place to show the gap not covered in these images.

 

Space News

Space News,

Observations of the centre of our galaxy over 20 years have shown the supermassive black hole “spaghettifying” a dust cloud, which has made it 100 times as long as the distance from Neptune to the Sun! The images were taken in infrared wavelengths because visible light is absorbed by materials near the centre, whereas IR is not. https://www.space.com/monster-black-hole-milky-way-shredding-mysterious-dust-cloud
