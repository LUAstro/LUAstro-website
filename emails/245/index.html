LUAstro - Solar Observing tomorrow!

This email was created with the campaign name: Solar Observing

This email was created with the purpose: Solar Observing (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 29/04/2026 to All 154 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello Astronomer!

We're going to be making the most of the great weather and will be running a Solar Observing event from 12:00 - 15:00 tomorrow (30/04/26). You can meet us outside the Physics Building at 12:00 where we'll head to Astrolab, or feel free to arrive later - the Physics Building will be fully open, so there's no need for us to let you in :)

We hope to see you there!

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
