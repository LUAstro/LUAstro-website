This email was sent on the 14/10/2025 to all 106 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello astronomer!

Our event for this week is an Introduction to Astronomy. We'll be giving a talk on the Society's equipment and the basic processes behind Astrophotography, followed by a tour of the Astrolab (and maybe a fun game on the Astrolab computers, we'll see). The event will be this Wednesday (15/10/25) at 19:00 in Furness Lecture Theatre 2 - we hope to see you all there!

Clear skies,

LUAstro

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
