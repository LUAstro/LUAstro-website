Hello Astronomers!

Tomorrow (12/02/25) at 7pm in the Margaret Fell Lecture Theatre we'll be running a fun Scavenger Hunt around campus! There will be prizes for the winning team! Although it's not set up to rain, it is Lancaster, so please bring a warm coat anyway!

We hope to see you there!
