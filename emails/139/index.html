LuAstroSoc: Week 2 Meeting + Observing

This email was created with the campaign name: Week 2

This email was created with the purpose: (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 16/10/2022 to all 155 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello, we will have our second meeting this Wednesday at 8pm in Fylde LT2. We will have a short presentation on space followed by a games night social. There will be some time as well for an observatory tour if you didn't manage to come last week.

If you're not sure where Fylde LT2 is, you may find this map helpful:  https://link.mazemap.com/EA5qCbDF



If you would like to come to an observing night, please sign up here if you haven't already done so: https://forms.gle/PMigUyBWJQTb4smC8

We will soon send out emails 10 people for the first observing night tomorrow. (We have a lot of people signed up already, which is fantastic! but it means it will take a while to get through everybody, so if you don't get an email yet don't worry). We will try and do as many observing session as we can, but it's very weather dependent. Once you have been to an observing night, you can submit the form again if you would like to come again. Last year we found it was very busy at the start of the term, but towards there was plenty of time for anybody to come when they wanted.

If you have any questions feel free to ask by emailing, or message us on any of our social media accounts (https://linktr.ee/luastrosoc). Also if you've not joined yet, here's our discord server link: https://discord.gg/rT6tH3H4

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
