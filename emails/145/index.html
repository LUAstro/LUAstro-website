Hey everyone! In this week’s newsletter we have some announcements about our next events and some photos we really want to share with you! 

 

Next Social (07/12)

Next Social (07/12),

For our next social tomorrow (07/12) we will have a presentation on astrophotography. We will be meeting in Furness LT1 at 8pm. Hope to see you there!

 

Observing Nights

Observing Nights,

The weather is looking great this week! Today (Tuesday), Wednesday and Thursday are looking very promising and maybe we can also do something on Friday so keep your eyes peeled on your emails as we might invite you for the next one! If you haven't signed up yet, you can click here and fill our form.

 

Photo gallery

Photo gallery,

These are the latest photos we have taken up in the observatory! They are pictures of the Horsehead Nebula and the Orion Nebula (M42), both in the constellation of Orion! 

 


 

Contacts
