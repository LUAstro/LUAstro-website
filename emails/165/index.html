This email was sent on the 08/10/2023 to all 47 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello everyone, thank you for signing up to LUAstro this year!

Welcome to all new members, and welcome back to those who have joined us before! 

We will run weekly events Thursdays 7pm as well as host observing nights when the sky is clear. Our plan for the year is below, however more information will be announced prior to all our events on insta/discord/emails. 

 

October:

12th - Taster session

19th - Astronomy 101

26th - Scavenger Hunt

November

2nd - Film Night

9th - Astrophotography 101

16th - Space Pirate Game

23rd - Star Systems

30th - Film Night: The Sequel

December

7th - Winter Event

14th - Quiz Night

 

 

Our first event this year is a taster/introductory session this Thursday (12th Oct) 7pm in Welcome Center LT2. There will be an introduction to the exec as well as more on what will happen throughout the year.

If you have any questions about anything, feel free to send us an email, or a message on discord or instagram.

 

Regards
LUAstro Exec
