This email was created with the campaign name: luastro merch 2

This email was created with the purpose: merch hoodie stuff (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 29/10/2021 to all 133 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello everyone! 

Through the post today I got the first sample of our merch! Since this is a first draft it's not complete, so we're looking for everyone's input on the design. As normal, I've put together a quick google form with a few sections about different aspects of the hoodie and would love if you could fill it out with some of your thoughts that would be great! There are photos of the jumper on the form and some more info about the design from the manufacturers (which we didn't know they could do but I think are really cool!) just to give you all a good idea about what you would be ordering.

https://forms.gle/uhQasWYRvQsUogva8

If you have any questions send us a message on socials (@luastrosoc) or by email

Thanks very much!

Jacob
