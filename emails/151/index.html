LY:MAN-α - Lancaster & York: Month of Astrophotography Nights 

LY:MAN-α, Lancaster & York: Month of Astrophotography Nights,

We are very excited to finally share with you that we are doing an astrophotography competition with York!

From 15th February to the 15th March, each society will take photos of night-sky objects within 5 categories:

1. In our Solar System

    This includes any object in our Solar System including (but not limited to) the Moon and our other planets. 

2. In the Milky Way                                                                                                                                   

    This includes objects such as globular clusters, open clusters and nebulae

3. Outside the Milky Way                                                                                                                         

    This is any object outside our own galaxy! This could be another galaxy, group or cluster of galaxies. 

4. The Rosette Nebula                                                                                                                               

    This being a competition with York, we thought it would be fitting to take a photo of a nebula resembling a rose. This is a large region of Hydrogen gas being lit up by a central collection of new stars!

5. With your phone!                                                                                                                                        

    This category is designed to be available to anyone! In this category you can take a picture of any object as long as you take them with your phone! 

 

After 15th March, the public will vote on the best photos and the society with the most votes will be announced as the winner on March 17th! Throughout this period we will be holding as many observing sessions as we can to get as many people helping with taking the photos, ensuring we get the best possible images!
