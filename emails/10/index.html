This email was sent on the 24/10/2018 to all 67 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi {{user_forename}}!

Apologies for a late email, this would normally have been on Monday, but I did not find the time to deliver this until now. It's week 3 of term and after a successful meteor hunt last week, we have found ourselves with clouds forecast tomorrow night. That sadly means that we won't be going out to the field or to the observatory.

However, our social sec has suggested we teach you a little about the varying types of telescope that are on offer, as well as making plans towards a movie night - we think week 7. So come along tomorrow to get your voice heard when it comes to choosing a space themed movie and answer in the straw-poll we will then make too! If you didn't come last week, do come along this week and say hello to our exec and everyone else.

And be prepared for another breath-taking powerpoint, made by yours truely,

-Antonio, president.
