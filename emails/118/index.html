LUAstro - Observing Week 20 + KSP Night

This email was sent on the 21/03/2022 to All 165 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi everyone, 

As you may have seen, we've now got our set-up to the point that we can get good photos! We took a photo of M51 (The Whirpool galaxy) as it passed overheard and managed to track it for 6 hours, getting data and then stacking it together to get a full colour photo. Tonight, we're planning on going up again to take a photo of the Crab Nebula (M1). We've tried this before but our process wasn't optimized like it is now! We'll also try for a globular cluster, which is a group of bound stars orbiting each other. They are relatively bright and should be easy to get good photos of. Please fill out the form below to come along tonight:

https://forms.gle/Zq2oZkGa5esaCJnR7

I've also heard back from Hoodie man and we have a price for all our hoodies so on Wednesday I'll put out the event where you can pay for your hoodie. We will just split the cost evenly between everyone so its fair between us all. The cost will be £17.40 per person.

Also this week, we will have our last social which will be a Kerbal Space Program Night! We will be in Margaret Fell LT at 7pm this Thursday, where we will have the game set up ready to play! For those that don't know, KSP is a simulator game that lets you build rockets and fly them an entire solar system which presents its own problems, but makes the successes much more satisfying! If things start going too badly, we'll step in and put everyone on the right course, but it might just be more fun to see how quickly everything can go to pot.

At this meeting, we'll also end out the form for Hustings next term. This will be for people who want to run for the exec of the society next year. If you are thinking of running, you don't have to fill out the form, this is just to give us an idea of the amount of interest people have for each role.

If you've got any questions please send us a message on socials (@luastrosoc) or join our Discord server:

https://discord.gg/am5HvWJS

Hope to see you all on Thursday!

Jacob
