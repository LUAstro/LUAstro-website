This email was sent on the 28/03/2022 to all 168 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi everyone 

As Michael said, thank you again to everyone for coming to our events we've held this term and please think about running for exec for next year! Since most people have now collected their hoodies from us (there's still a few left so let us know next term when you're free) we'd like to collect payments for the company that made them for us. Prices are on the website which can be found through the instructions below.

To pay, please head to the SU website and search for 'LUAstro' in the search bar. Then head to 'Events' and you should see the hoodie payment screen. We can only pay the company as soon as everyone has paid the SU so please complete this as quickly as possible. 
Remember, this is only if you reserved a hoodie before!

If you've got any questions please feel free to send us a message

See everyone next term!

Jacob
