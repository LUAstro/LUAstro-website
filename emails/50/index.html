Elections and Movie Night

This email was created with the campaign name: Elections and Movie Night!

This email was created with the purpose: (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 26/02/2020 to all 80 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi {{user_forename}}!

It is fair to say that, as a society, we have not been very active this term. And that is not without reason. However, the society's current exec will not be continuing to the new year. The society needs to fill three roles to survive:

-President, who represents the society to the student's union, coordinate the exec and society.

-Secretary, who takes notes at exec meetings, manages society social media, ensures safety regulations are met.

-Treasurer, who manages finance (typically none at the moment, but new exec may wish to take a new aspect on things)

It is not compulsory, but it would certainly help if at least 1 member of the exec are studying Physics, as this will make to much easier to arrange access to the observatory in the Physics dept. If you wish to take on management of the society, please attend on Thursday 5th March at 7pm in Welcome Center LT3. If you wish to see a better society for 2020/21, please also attend to vote! Candidates for each role should prepare a short speech on why they want to take on the role, as well as what changes they may want to make to the society in the coming year(s). After that, we will have a presentation a guest speaker, Keenan Wright, on something Astronomy! 

In other news, we will be hosting our usual movie night in week 20. That's Thursday 19th March at 7pm, Welcome Center LT3. To vote on your chosen movie please use this strawpoll: http://www.strawpoll.me/19448386

 

Many thanks,

-Antonio
