LUAstro - Quiz 2/12

This email was created with the campaign name: luastro quiz night

This email was created with the purpose: tell people about quiz (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 29/11/2021 to All 145 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello everyone!

This week, we're holding a quiz! We will be in Fylde LT 2 at 7pm on Thursday 2nd December. We'll have a few rounds of questions on general knowledge, film and TV, science (of course!) and finally a Marvel round! There will be a prize for the winning team as well, so hopefully that's a bit of incentive to win! Please feel free to bring along other people to the quiz, then we'll have more teams and make it a bit more interesting!

If you have any questions please send us a message on socials (@luastrosoc) or join our Discord server:

https://discord.gg/F9YrHcVE

See you Thursday!

Jacob

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
