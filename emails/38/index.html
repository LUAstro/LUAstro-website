Astronomy Society - Membership 2019/20?!

This email was created with the campaign name: previous members

This email was sent on the 03/10/2019 to including users belongs to all of the user groups : Alumni--Astronomy Society (LUAstroSoc)--Group 121 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi {{user_forename}}!

We thought you might like to be a member of the society again this year! All you have to do is sign up for free on our LUSU page! 

This year we are aiming high with the society, and have lots of plans for events during the year, some of which we cannot reveal just yet, but we are very, very excited to carry out these plans! One of our plans involves the transit of Mercury that will occur on the afternoon of the 11th November - on our YouTube channel (SUBSCRIBE!), we will be livestreaming the entire event should the weather be in our favour, and will later upload a timelapse of the transit. We will be testing the livestreaming set-up in the coming weeks with live views of the Moon when it reappears in the evening skies. Fingers crossed the weather improves a little throughout the next month or so! So don't miss out! Subscribe and hit the bell icon to be notified of when we go live!

Our monthly articles featuring the highlights of Astronomy will continue throughout the year. This month's can be found here, where we detail some basic ideas in Astronomy and give suggestions for some mobile phone astrophotography. On a related note, we are hosting an astrophotography contest, in which the three best pictures submitted will win the photographer an astro based prize. There will be more details on this during our first meeting at 7pm on Thursday 10th October in Welcome Center LT3. It is in here every subsequent meeting will take place. We have a few astrophotography projects in mind ourselves, such as star trails, a collection of all the Moon's phases, Saturn, Jupiter, Uranus, the Andromeda Galaxy, and more!

This year we have better access of the large telescope and observatory to aid with these projects - and yes, for the astrophotography contest you can use these telescopes to get your pictures during our sessions! Last year it was difficult to obtain a key for the dome, however the director of the observatory, Dr John Stott, has allowed LUAstro the chance to sort out an old 11 inch telescope that has previously been sat in a box. This will not be locked in the dome, and so is more accessible to the society. While this telescope is 3 inches smaller than the main observatory, this one will be much easier for the society to set up and pack away and can be moved about the rooftop as necessary, allowing for observations of areas of the sky hidden from the dome.

So we highly recommend you sign up for the Astronomy Society (it is free, after all), and come to our first meeting on 10th October @7pm in Welcome Center LT3. And we highly recommend you follow our Twitter and like the Facebook page if you haven't already for more frequent updates/posts than by email.

We hope to see you soon,

-Antonio
