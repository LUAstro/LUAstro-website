This email was sent on the 13/10/2019 to All 55 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi {{user_forename}}!

Looking at the weather for the week does not promising, and as of yet we are not expecting any observations this Thursday's meeting. This week we will be giving a little presentation on some of the basics in Astronomy, including what different objects there are to observe, how to locate objects in the night sky, how to operate a telescope, and more! We hope next week we can put this learning to good use, as so far the 14 day weather forecast looks promising. Got any burning questions or anything else Astronomy you want to know? Just message the Facebook, Twitter, email us on our new email address "astronomy.su@groups.lancaster.ac.uk", or come speak to us on Thursday!

In terms of the space program, things seem to be going well. We have spoken with the LUSU Societies team who are on board with the idea, and have full training in preparation for the Moon landing. We now simply have to wait on facilities to accept the risks involved and arrange an outdoor space for us - more details will come on that nearer the time.

We already have submissions for our astrophotography contest this term. Remember, your pictures do not have to have been taken in one of our sessions, but they do have to have been taken by yourself. You can find out more in the image below:

We hope to see you on Thursday at 7pm in Welcome Center LT3, and we certainly hope the weather takes an unexpected turn for the better by then. We also hope to see your astrophotos during the term!

-Antonio
