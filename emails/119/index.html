This email was sent on the 23/03/2022 to All 167 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi everyone

Tonight will be another Observing night to try and take some photos with the 11" telescope. Since it's a bit short notice and will be held at 10pm tonight, please send Orlando an email if you're interested in going along(o.prugel-bennett@lancaster.ac.uk). 

Tomorrow night we will also have our final social of the term, the KSP night! Kerbal Space Program is a game that includes a custom solar system and allows you to build rockets to travel to different planets in that system.

See my previous email for more details about that. We will also be talking a bit about hustings next term to be part of the exec of the society into next year, so please come along if you're interested in that.

See you then!

Jacob
