This email was sent on the 09/06/2023 to all 237 LUAstro members at the time .

Hey everyone! Hope all your exams and assignments are going well! Here is a few announcements for this week!

★ Our social this week is gonna be a presentation by a guest speaker all the way from University of Porto, Portugal Prof. Henrique Salgado! He is a lecturer at the Engineering Department in Porto and has a great interest in astronomy. This interest has led him into astrophotography in which he has taken several brilliant photos. He will tell us all about the tips and tricks and all the steps to take great photos of our sky!

Come along to this talk on Friday 9th June at 8pm on Fylde LT2. Hope to see you there!

★ The weather this past couple of weeks has been really nice and although hours of nighttime are reducing, I am sure that we can arrange some observing nights so keep your eyes peeled for it!

★ We also have some posters in the making that you can get for 1£ so if you are interested just let reach out to us!

That was it for this week! Have a lovely week and clear skies everyone! ⭐️

Best wishes, 

LUAstro exec

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
