This email was sent on the 14/10/2023 to all 78 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello again,
It's another clear night, so I'll head to the observatory to do some more imaging.
Again, this isn't an official observing night and will be about the same as yesterday, with not too much to do, but if anyone would like to join you are once again all welcome. There will still be plenty of official observing nights coming up, so you won't be missing out if you don't come today.

Just as yesterday, if you would like to join, please come to the front of physics building on campus (by the sliding doors) and then ping/dm me (@orlando) on discord so I can let you into the building. If you don't have discord, you can also send me an email o.prugel-benentt@lancaster.ac.uk or dm the luastrosoc instagram. I will again be there from 20:45 onward, feel free to come any time. Please also bring some warm clothes, as it get's quite cold on clear nights.

- Orlando
