LUAstro - Astrophotography Workshop

This email was created with the campaign name: Astrophotography workshop

This email was created with the purpose: Astrophotography Workshop (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 12/01/2025 to All 172 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello Astronomers!

Our first event of 2025 is an Astrophotography Workshop this Wednesday (15/01/25) at 7pm in the Astrolab!

We will be giving an interactive presentation on processing astronomical data using the free software Siril, which will allow you to create lovely images! Data of the Horsehead Nebula will be provided for practice. Due to limitations with the Astrolab computers, you will have to bring a laptop with Siril and StarNet downloaded ahead of time. You may also be interested in further using GraXpert to denoise images. Also, try to arrive a little before 7pm so we have time to let you up!

We hope to see you there!

LUAstro

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
