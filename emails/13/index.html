Astronomy Week 6 & IOP Trip to Alston Observatory

This email was created with the campaign name: iop trip

This email was created with the purpose: (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 13/11/2018 to All 77 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi {{user_forename}}

This week we will be attempting to go for another meteor spot for those who may have missed them last time after the meeting on Thursday 15th. If the weather is unfavourable, I will be giving a short presentation about star charts and how to use them, as well as some of the different constellations and how to spot them. If you are interested in either, we will be in Bowland North Seminar Room 23 from 7pm. 

We also have a planned movie night on the 22nd November. This will replace our usual meeting on the day and will take place from 7pm in Elizabeth Livingston LT. The movie we will be screening is Apollo 13 and will finish by around 9.30. You can find out more information and show your interest on the Facebook Event Page. 

Another event that is happening on 23rd November will be an IOP organised trip/tour of the Alston Observatory, just outside of Preston. There will be a minibus from the Underpass at 6.30pm, to arrive there by 8pm and the tour lasting approximately 1hr30m. You can find out more information by clicking here. The event is free and open to all, you just have to create a quick log on when booking tickets - but be quick, there are only 20 left!

Thanks,

-Antonio, president.
