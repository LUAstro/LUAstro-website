LUAstro Movie Night

This email was created with the campaign name: Movie Night

This email was created with the purpose: (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 01/03/2018 to all 32 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello {{user_forename}}!

There wasn't any observations this week, due to the snow and an icy rooftop, but next week we have a movie night planned. We're showing Gravity from 6pm onwards in Elizabeth Livingston LT on Wednesday 7th March. We have said that you can bring food/drink, but request that no rubbish/mess be left behind, or we won't be able to book rooms again (we're not technically allowed food/drink in there). You can find more details and updates on the Facebook page, but if you don't have Facebook, some emails will be sent out to update should the event be cancelled for any reason. We do only have limited spacing though, but it is quite a large lecture theatre, so it shouldn't be too much of a problem. 

What is the film about? You can read the synopsis of the film here:

Dr. Ryan Stone (Sandra Bullock) is a medical engineer on her first shuttle mission. Her commander is veteran astronaut Matt Kowalsky (George Clooney), helming his last flight before retirement. Then, during a routine space walk by the pair, disaster strikes: The shuttle is destroyed, leaving Ryan and Matt stranded in deep space with no link to Earth and no hope of rescue. As fear turns to panic, they realize that the only way home may be to venture further into space.

We hope to see you there!
