This email was sent on the 29/11/2018 to All 80 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi {{user_forename}}!

Tonight at 7pm in Bowland North Seminar Room 23, I will be presenting a little about space exploration, given the recent landing of NASA's InSight lander. Sadly, due to the weather we won't be going out doing any observations, but you are more than welcome to come along regardless. 

Next week, I would recommend you come as we have a Quiz night planned, with a Kahoot Quiz, a crossword speed puzzle and more - with a scoring system and Prizes! 

And in Week 10 we will have the charity fundraiser for Lancashire Mind. We have sent an email about it previously, and we recommend you come along if you are interested in Galaxies and Black Holes! Tickets cost £1 and all are welcome. Please invite your friends, but buy them tickets :) You can find out more information on the Facebook Event Page and on our LUSU page.

Sorry for the late email :( 

-Antonio
