Hi {{user_forename}}!

Tomorrow from 7pm in Welcome Center LT3 we will begin our series of presentations that cover the general lifetime of a star and the Universe. Below are the titles of the presentations. After each presentation we will be attempting observations that reflect the topic each week. Each presentation shouldn't last longer than 30 mins, with a hopeful hour or so of observations. Feel free to bring your own telescope each week if you have one, I will be bringing mine, though we will email about the weather forecast on the day suggesting if you probably shouldn't bring it.

7pm in Welcome Center LT3,

I'd also like to thank everyone who braved the cold and poor forecast to observe the Lunar Eclipse with us. We were blessed by the Astronomy Gods and got some brilliant images, some of which are on our Twitter @lancasterastro. Our Twitter has some more personalised posts from myself, will share other people's images, and space news, while the Facebook page will be kept to be more informative and sharing some specifics - so we highly recommend following our Twitter!

I hope to see you tomorrow,

-Antonio
