Hi {{user_forename}}!

Tomorrow at 7pm in Welcome Center LT3 we are having a seminar on Galaxies! This should last only half an hour or so, though sadly it seems tomorrow will be cloudy so we will not be observing. We hope next Thursday is clearer, as we will attempt observations of everything we have looked at this term, from planets to binary stars, nebulae to galaxies! Failing that, we will try to arrange for a long access to the observatory one night in the coming weeks.

In week 11 we will be having a movie night, there is a strawpoll for the choice of movie, you can find this here: https://www.strawpoll.me/17475687

We hope to see you tomorrow, and for the seminar, and we hope to have good news about observatory use in the coming weeks!

-Antonio
