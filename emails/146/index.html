Next Social (14/11),

Observing sessions,

Photo gallery

Photo gallery,

​​

Photo taken from https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap221206.html

This is the 6th November APOD (Astronomy Picture of the Day) showing the Pillars of Creation in detail Eagle Nebula (M16). This was taken by JWST (James Webb Space Telescope) in near-infrared light. It is currently birthing stars and it appears solid because of its high dust content and great depth although it is very low in density.
