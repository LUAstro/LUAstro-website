LUAstro - Meeting 14/10 correction

This email was created with the campaign name: Meeting 14/10

This email was created with the purpose: Correcting some details about meeting (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 13/10/2021 to including users belongs to any of the user groups : Current Members--Astronomy Society (LUAstroSoc)--Group 102 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello everyone!

We have our first meeting tomorrow evening at 7pm. We had originally planned to observe Saturn and the Moon (which appear very close at the moment), but unfortunately we won't be able to meet in the Physics department as we haven't had the full go-ahead from the Health and Safety people in Physics. Instead, we have a meeting at 7pm in Fylde lecture theatre 3. This is along the South Spine near the Maths and Statistics building.

The meeting will consist of a bit of an intro about the society, the equpiment we have access to, and plans for the year ahead, so please make sure you're there! If we have time and people are up for it, we could go through some basic astronomy and space science just to start getting people up to speed on the types of things we might end up seeing through this year.

Once again, if anyone has any questions about the meeting or anything generally, feel free to message us on social media (@LUAstrosoc on Twitter and Instagram) or if you've joined Discord send us a message in a channel there or dm us. If you haven't joined already, Discord is a great way to quickly get in contact and also talk freely to all the other members of the society, the link to the server is below:

https://discord.gg/fWyjApCU

Thanks very much, hopefully see you all tomorrow!

Jacob
