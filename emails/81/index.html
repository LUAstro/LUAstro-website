LUAstro - First Meeting

This email was created with the campaign name: First Meeting

This email was created with the purpose: Organise first meet-up (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 11/10/2021 to including users belongs to any of the user groups : Current Members--Astronomy Society (LUAstroSoc)--Group 87 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello everyone!

Welcome to the first LUAstro email for this year. This will be a bit longer than normal as there is a little bit more stuff to do so we're all set up.

1. We have set up a Discord server! If anyone hasn't used this before, don't worry it's not too difficult to get used to. It's a bit like Teams, but with seperate channels for text and video/voice. If we had a meeting online, Discord is much better suited for handling groups of people and sharing screens quickly, plus we can have a bit more freedom with accounts and that sort of thing. Accounts can be created for free by going to discord.com and also downloading the software (it is also free through iOS and Android). Then the link to our server is below:

https://discord.gg/mYTcFj86

This link should work for the next 7 days, but please get in contact either through email, or on social media and I'll send out another link

2. We are hopefully holding a meeting this Thursday! We will aim to meet at the Astrolab (D floor in the Physics building, turn right as you come out from the stairs/elevator) at 7pm on Thursday 14/10. Saturn and the Moon are together in the sky, and so we are aiming to have a good look at both of them, and maybe even see some of the moons around Saturn. Weather is looking a bit cloudy at the moment, but this could all change. If, either the forecast on Wednesday doesn't look good, or come Thursday it seems like it will be cloudy, we will look at booking another room and meeting their instead, where we can have a short presentation on a topic

Sorry this ended up being quite a lengthy email, there was still a fair bit to sort out so I've just put it all in this email. Any questions, please get in touch. We are on Twitter and Instagram at @LUAstroSoc, and on Facebook under LUAstro

Hopefully see you all Thursday evening!

Jacob

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
