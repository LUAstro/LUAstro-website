LUAstro - Star Trek Night!

This email was created with the campaign name: Star Trek Night

This email was created with the purpose: Star Trek Night (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 05/11/2024 to all 153 LUAstro members at the time .

Hello Astronomers!

This week we will be watching The Wrath of Khan in the Cavendish lecture theatre. After the film, there will be a brief presentation about the real stars featured in the franchise, and we may be able to do some observing in the Astrolab, weather permitting.The film will commence at 7pm, so it'd be best to arrive before then.

We hope to see you there!
