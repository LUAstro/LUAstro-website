This email was sent on the 11/11/2022 to All 201 LUAstro members at the time .


 

For our next social we will be watching Black Panther: Wakanda Forever! 

We will watch it at 8:10pm (screen 3) on Wednesday 16th in Vue Cinema, Lancaster.

To make things simple, you will have to book your own tickets via the Vue
Website.
(https://www.myvue.com/book-tickets/summary/10061/250283/170297)

The exec will be booking tickets in row G. For those living on campus, the exec will meet by the underpass at 7:25pm. 

 

Saturday 12th is looking quite clear so will host an observing night for everybody to come along to. Please meet outside the physics building (opposite Subway) at 6pm. We will likely be going to one of the fields around campus for observing, so bring some warm clothes, and be prepared to possibly get a bit muddy. If anybody has their own equipment (telescopes ect…) feel free to bring it.

We will continue to run our normal observing nights with the big telescopes whenever possible, so if you’re not yet signed up you can do so here. Monday is also looking clear for now so we might observe again then.

 

Please complete this anonymous feedback form if you’ve got any suggestions for the society:  https://forms.gle/488vg3HDW4fodeXW7
