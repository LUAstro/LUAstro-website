LUAstro - Final Meeting 21/22

This email was created with the campaign name: luastro kenobi last meeting

This email was created with the purpose: (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 20/06/2022 to All 167 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi everyone 

This week we'll be watching the finale to Kenobi! We'll be in Furness LT 1 this Wednesday at 7pm to watch. 

This will also be the last meeting for this year! From all the exec we wanted to say a big thank you to everyone for getting involved with all the events and observing nights we've held, and hope you've enjoyed being a part of this society as much as we have had running it! We'll of course be back for next year, with a new group of people running the show. We'll have more information about the new year once we've figured it all out but expect more observing nights as the planets become visible during the night time.

If you want, you can join our Discord sever below, and post some cool pictures in our Astrophotography section or link any cool videos you find in the Recommendations section:

https://discord.gg/eaDtw5Uk

Thanks all for coming along, and see you Wednesday!

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
