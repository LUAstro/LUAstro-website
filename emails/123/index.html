LUAstro - Welcome back and Hustings

This email was created with the campaign name: luastro hustings

This email was created with the purpose: (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 25/04/2022 to All 169 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi everyone

Welcome back to third term! Since it's the first week back there's a few bits of info to get out to people:

1) We will be hosting a hustings meeting this Wednesday at 7pm in Furness LT 1. Hustings is where we decide who will be on the exec for next year, so if you like to run for that please come along! Equally if you're not planning to run but want a say in the future of the society, come along to vote for some of the candidates. Michael put together a great document outlining all the roles available to people which I will link below, so please have a look at that.

https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dnrhnbpHjfENmha0mtLLIgY70pOEMOjb/view?usp=sharing

Since we're not expecting hustings to take too long, we'll also have a presentation for afterwards

2) Observing will carry on as normal going into this term. However, we are very aware that people have exams going on and won't be able to attend all if any the events. We will run as many observing nights as we can, obviously these are very weather dependent and dependent on Accredited Observers being available but none the less, we will try our best! Having said that, the weather is looking pretty bad for this week so we won't be releasing a form for this week.

3) We've had an email from Physics admissions asking us to put together a few items for the new Physics brochure for upcoming year! We'll send them some of our most recent photos and a little description of the society. They have also requested a photo of the society to go with the description! If you would like to be in this photo there will be a photoshoot day happening on 10th May. I will find out more details about timings but we will get one outside the dome with all of our hoodies on!

That was a lot of information in one go, so please don't hesitate to send us an email or find us on socials (@luastrosoc).

Hopefully see you on Wednesday to find our new exec!

Jacob

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
