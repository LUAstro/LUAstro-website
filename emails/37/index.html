Observation suggestions for Physics undergrad trip to Las Palmas

This email was created with the campaign name: las palmas trip

This email was created with the purpose: (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 12/06/2019 to all 92 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi {{user_forename}}!

I hope exams are going or have gone well :) I have a proposal today involving a Physics undergrad trip to the Roque de los Muchachos Observatory in La Palmas. The team of 3, who sadly who have already been chosen - I cannot offer this position to you guys! - have the opportunity to use the Isaac Newton Telescope for research and general observations during their stay next week!

The purpose of this email is to ask members of the society if they have any suggestions for observing targets. Please bear in mind that it must be a Summer object visible from the Canary Islands and that these targets may vary slightly from that of here in Lancaster. Should you want to search for what is visible, there are many online tools that can help determine this such as https://in-the-sky.org/skymap.php, which can work for any location on Earth - just change the location to La Palmas by clicking the islands off the coast of Morocco. You can then check the desired object's constellation by cross checking with other online sources (wikipedia page for that object is good enough) and see if that is anywhere on the planetarium when the time is set to be night. 

If you have any ideas please let us know by replying to this email and I will forward this on to the team :) And if you want updates at a later date, all details will be on a blog https://xgalweb.wordpress.com/observing/ and details from previous year's trips are there too! 

Thanks

-Antonio
