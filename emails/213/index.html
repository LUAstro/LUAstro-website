Welcome to LUAstro!

This email was created with the campaign name: Freshers' Fair Announcement

This email was created with the purpose: Freshers' Fair Announcement (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 02/10/2025 to All 21 LUAstro members at the time .

Good evening,

I hope you enjoyed Freshers' Fair today, and picked up plenty of free goodies! We'd like to thank you for having joined LUAstro.

Next Wednesday (08/10/2025), at 19:00 in the Cavendish Lecture Theatre, we will be holding an introductory talk about the society, a fun quiz, and a quick academic talk, with the latter being given by none other than the Physics Department's Dr. Mathew Smith. Mathew is a lecturer and researcher, who specialises in Observational Astrophysics; in particular, using type 1a supernovae as standard candles for determining the expansion history of the Universe.

We also have packed for those of you who are studying Physics, keep an eye out on Friday, as we may or may not be making a surprise appearance... Whilst October in Lancaster is not particularly conducive to clear skies, we will be hosting an observing night as soon as the clouds allow. In the meantime, make sure you're in our Discord server and visit our new website if you haven't already. We look forward to meeting you properly!

Kind regards,

The LUAstro Executive

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
