Recent observing night pics!

Here are some photos we took at the first clear nights this term...

First up is the Andromeda Galaxy, along with two of its dwarf satellite galaxies. The galaxy is twice as large as our own, with 10 times the number of stars and an even more massive supermassive black hole at the center.

Next is the Pleiades, an open cluster of stars moving through a cloud of gas and dust in interstellar space. The stars rip at the dust with gravity, push it with radiation, creating the structures you can see here.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Last we have Jupiter, with an eclipse... its moon Io cast a shadow on the planet, which we saw moving over the course of the night! Eclipses are quite common on Jupiter because it has lots of moons and is so large compared to Earth.

As always, you can message us on discord or instagram if you have any questions.
