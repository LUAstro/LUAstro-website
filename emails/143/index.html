This email was sent on the 22/11/2022 to All 208 LUAstro members at the time .

For our next meeting, on Wednesday 23rd Joe will be doing a presentation on History and Future of Lunar missions in Furness LT1 at 8pm.

The weather is looking pretty bad (as always), however Tuesday night might be okay. Keep an eye on our social media and discord as we may announce a last minute observing session. In the meantime, sign up here

 

Artemis 1 finally launched the Orion Spacecraft last week, sending back many great photos!

 

Webb took a picture of a protostar, which will eventually undergo fusion! In this image the small horizontal line in the centre is a planetary disc, as wide as our solar system, made from heavier elements that will form planets. The dust and gas above and below the protostar is from material ejected from it many years ago.

 

A NASA demonstration of an inflatable heat shield was successful and can be scaled up for missions on Earth and Mars, project leaders said Nov. 17.
