LUAstro - Photo Day and Good-bye from the old exec

This email was created with the campaign name: luastro end of 21/22 exec + photo day

This email was created with the purpose: (this is usually left blank)

This email was sent on the 29/04/2022 to All 169 LUAstro members at the time .

Hi everyone,

Just a quick update and a final thank you and good-bye from Michael and Jacob!

On May 10th, Physics are doing a photoshoot for the new prospectus and have asked us if we'd like to have our photo taken, so if you'd like to take part please fill out the form included below to confirm beforehand who will be going. We'll close the form at 12pm tomorrow, so then we have a definite list of people going. If you have one, please bring your LUAstro hoodie along with you!

https://forms.gle/owmXtbc5QNyC7Kki8

Finally, as we get to the end of this year the current exec will be stepping back and a new set of people will be leading the society into next year. We wanted to say a massive thank you to everyone who has come along to our meetings, signed up for observing nights and bought hoodies. We've gone from weekly Teams meetings with little presentations to regular Observing nights from November this year, getting hoodies ordered and getting the 11" CST fully up and running and capturing some amazing photos of galaxies, nebulae and even the International Space Station! Orlando, MJ and Joe will be your new President, Secretary and Social Secretary for the rest of this year and into next year. Both Michael and Jacob will still be around but will be stepping down from their positions and assuming more helper roles in the society. The new team have some great ideas and we're really excited to see what next year brings.

Thanks again to everyone and clear skies!

Jacob and Michael

Lancaster University Astronomy Society

LUAstro observes the wonders of space and the stars all from the top of the Physics Building! Join us for observing nights, Astrophotography workshops, socials, and more!
